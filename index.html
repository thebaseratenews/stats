How flags are selected (pre-registered)

Selection is mechanical — fixed rules, zero discretion, quiet days included:

  • F1 Earnings gap: every S&P 500 earnings gap of 3% or more at the 9:25 ET snapshot, up or down.
  • F2 Rarity: any F1 gap in the top decile of the trailing four quarters, with its explicit rank.
  • F1-cluster: any sector contributing three or more flags in one day.
  • Macro calendar: every scheduled US release (CPI, PPI, NFP, GDP, PCE, Retail Sales, and related).
  • Market structure: daily SPY/sector statistics with percentile-gated call-outs, history since 2000.

Statistics rules: full distributions, never win rates; explicit N everywhere; no slice published below N=100; coarse public-knowledge buckets only. Historical base rates cover 2020–present; the universe skews to current index constituents (survivorship caveat). Forward returns are measured from the flag day's open — the first price available after the gap is observable.
